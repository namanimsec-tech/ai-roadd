Appendix II

Idea-Teaching Algorithms

Not industry-critical today, but skipping them leaves a conceptual gap. Treat as Tier 3, but non-negotiable.

AlgorithmWhy It's Kept
PerceptronTeaches the linear-separability limit → motivates MLPs
Vanilla RNNTeaches the vanishing-gradient limit → motivates LSTM → motivates Attention
Hidden Markov ModelsTeaches sequential / latent structure before attention existed
GANsTeaches adversarial training before diffusion dominance
Rule-Based Expert SystemsTeaches why pure symbolic AI didn't scale → motivates statistical learning, and later, neuro-symbolic hybrids
Normalizing Flows / Energy-Based ModelsTeach alternative density-modeling views that clarify what diffusion models are actually doing
